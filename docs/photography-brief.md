# Photography & copy brief

For whoever shoots and writes the product content. Written against what the
site actually renders, so nothing here is decorative.

## The problem this solves

Every product image today is a jar on white. The jars are handsome, but nobody
buys a jar — they buy dinner. **The single biggest visual gap is food in
context**, and no amount of design work substitutes for it.

## Context

**Brand** — Efamy Food Products. Ghanaian chilli sauces and seasonings, made in
the UK. Trading 15+ years, new to selling direct.

**Range** — seven protein-based chilli sauces in mild and hot (beans, beef,
chicken, fish, goat, pork, turkey), plus All Purpose Seasoning, Kelewele
Seasoning and Coat & Cook. Sauces come in 175g, 250g, 500g and 800g.

**Customers** — UK households cooking Ghanaian and West African food, the
diaspora buying what they grew up with, and cooks discovering it. Plus
independent retailers.

**Personality** — premium, warm, authentic, confident. Not supermarket-bright,
not folksy, not a market stall.

## Shot list per product

Six per SKU. The site has slots for all of them today.

| # | Shot | Where it appears | Notes |
| - | ---- | ---------------- | ----- |
| 1 | **Packshot, background removed** | Product cards, PLP, basket | Already have these. Square, jar centred, even lighting, label readable |
| 2 | **Hero in context** | Product page gallery, first slot | The jar on a worktop beside the meal it belongs to. This is the one that sells |
| 3 | **Texture close-up** | Gallery | The sauce itself — spooned, glossy, chilli flecks visible. Macro |
| 4 | **Ingredients** | Gallery | Scotch bonnet, onion, garlic, ginger around the open jar. Supports the short ingredient list |

**Optional:** 10–20s vertical video of a spoonful being lifted or stirred
through rice. Mobile hero only.

## Dish photography

Separate from products, used by the meal pairings row and recipe pages. **Nine
dishes needed; three exist.**

Kenkey & fish · Jollof rice · Grilled chicken · Waakye · Yam & plantain ·
Kelewele · Rice & stew · Spaghetti · Fried eggs

Square, cropped tight on the plate — the tiles are circular, so anything outside
the plate is discarded.

## Style

**Light** — soft and directional, as if from a window. Natural shadow. No ring
light, no flat overhead flash.

**Surfaces** — warm wood, stone, matte ceramic. The site sits on warm ivory, so
saturated backdrops fight both the palette and the labels. **Avoid strong
greens and blues**, which clash with the jars.

**Props** — minimal and real. Whole scotch bonnets, garlic, a wooden spoon, a
cloth. No styled clutter.

**Grading** — consistent across every product. True colour. The labels are the
brand's colour system; if grading shifts them, the site stops matching the
product.

**Do not** — use generic stock, composite a jar into a scene it was not in, or
use AI-generated food. A customer receiving something that looks nothing like
the photograph is a refund and a bad review.

## Copy per product

**Title** — as printed on the jar. `Beef Chilli Sauce — Hot`. The site adds
size and heat around it; do not repeat them in the name.

**Summary** — one line, under 80 characters, shown on cards. Taste and occasion,
not ingredients.

> Rich, slow-cooked beef with a heat that stays with you.

**Description** — two or three short paragraphs on the product page:

1. **Taste and texture.** What it actually tastes like. Heat, depth, what comes
   first and what lingers.
2. **How to use it.** The meals it belongs to and how much to use.
3. **How it is made.** Small batch, no artificial preservatives, made in the UK
   — only what is true.

**Best paired with** — three to five dishes. These render as chips and feed the
pairings row.

## Facts still needed

The site has fields for all of these and they are empty. Each is a question a
customer asks before buying.

- [ ] **Net weights** for the seven products missing them
- [ ] **Nutrition per 100g** — none exists for any product
- [ ] **Ingredients** — only Pork and Coat & Cook are transcribed
- [ ] **Allergens** — verify against every physical jar
- [ ] **Storage and shelf life** — currently one line copied from the Pork label
- [ ] **Certifications** — SALSA, BRC, organic, halal? If any exist they belong
      on the product page
- [ ] **Delivery cost, timescale, and any free-delivery threshold**

**Ingredients and allergens must be transcribed from the jars, never guessed.**
Wrong allergen data on food is a safety and legal issue, not a copy error.

## Delivery checklist

Per product:

- [ ] 6 images, longest edge ≥ 2000px, sRGB
- [ ] Cut-out packshot on transparent background
- [ ] Title, summary, three-paragraph description
- [ ] Ingredients, allergens, storage, shelf life
- [ ] Net weight for every size sold
- [ ] Three to five pairings

Per dish:

- [ ] One square image, ≥ 1200px, tight on the plate

Deliver as JPEG or PNG at full size. The site handles compression and format
conversion.
