# Information needed from Efamy

Everything the website is waiting on. Written to be sent to the client as-is —
no technical language, grouped so it can be answered in stages.

**Nothing here can be guessed.** Several items are legal requirements for
selling food online, and the rest are questions customers ask before buying.
Where an answer is missing, the site currently hides that section rather than
inventing an answer.

## 1. Blocking launch

The shop cannot take an order until these exist.

### Payments

- [ ] A **Stripe account in the business's legal name**, with the business bank
      details. This cannot be set up by anyone else — it needs the company
      details, a bank account and ID verification.

### Delivery

- [ ] **What does delivery cost?** A flat rate, or by weight or basket value?
- [ ] **How long does it take?** For example "2 to 3 working days".
- [ ] **Is there a free delivery threshold?** For example free over £30. Very
      effective at increasing order size, but only if it is real.
- [ ] **Which countries?** UK only, or Ireland and Europe as well?
- [ ] **How are orders sent?** Royal Mail, courier, something else?

### Legal pages

The Privacy, Terms and Returns pages are **drafts and are marked as such on the
site**. They follow the shape UK law requires but have not been checked by
anyone qualified.

- [ ] Have all three reviewed by someone qualified
- [ ] **Returns for food** are not straightforward: sealed and perishable food
      carries specific exemptions from the usual 14-day right to cancel. This
      needs a proper answer, not a copied policy.

## 2. Product information

The site has a place for all of this. Each empty field is a question a customer
asks before buying.

**Never estimate these.** Wrong allergen information on food is a safety and
legal matter.

- [ ] **Ingredients for every product.** Only Pork Chilli Sauce and Coat & Cook
      are done. Ten products have none.
- [ ] **Allergens for every product**, checked against the jar in hand.
- [ ] **Net weight for every size.** Seven products currently show no weight at
      all.
- [ ] **Storage instructions and shelf life**, for example "12 months
      unopened".

### Helpful

- [ ] **Nutrition per 100g.** Nothing exists for any product.
- [ ] **Certifications** — SALSA, BRC, organic, halal, anything else? Fifteen

      non-chilli are still made, they are missing from the shop entirely — and
      the non-chilli version in particular is the one that sells to families.

## 3. A pricing query

The trade price list has something that looks like a typo:

| Beans 800g  | Price      | Per jar   |
| ----------- | ---------- | --------- |
| 6-pack      | £68.00     | £11.33    |
| **12-pack** | **£66.00** | **£5.50** |
| 24-pack     | £130.00    | £5.42     |

**The 12-pack is cheaper than the 6-pack.** Every other line holds a steady per
jar price across pack sizes — Beef 800g is £12.33 a jar whether you buy 6, 12 or 24. Beans 800g should probably be about £136 and £271.

This is trade pricing so it does not appear on the website, but it is worth
checking before it goes to a wholesale customer.

## 6. Decisions

Not information, but choices only the business can make.

- [ ] **Subscriptions?** Chilli sauce is a repeat purchase, so a "subscribe and
      save 10%" option is a strong fit. It changes the checkout, so it is worth
      deciding early.
- [ ] **Multipacks or gift sets for customers?** The site supports them. Which
      sets, and at what price?
- [ ] **Who holds the `efamys.co.uk` domain?** You already own it and the new
      site will use it, so nothing needs buying. We just need the login for
      wherever it is registered, so the new site can be pointed at it. That
      account should be in the business's name — it controls both the website
      and the `info@efamys.co.uk` email.
      This also fixes a real problem: some UK mobile networks block the
      temporary web address entirely, so the site is unreachable on them.
